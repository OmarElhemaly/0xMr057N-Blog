## Why this blog?

Welcome to **0xMr057N | Security Research** — my public notebook for learning, testing, and documenting cybersecurity.

The goal is simple: understand how vulnerabilities work, why they happen, how to validate them safely, and how to prevent them.

## What I'll publish

- **Web security:** practical notes on common and advanced web vulnerabilities.
- **Writeups:** walkthroughs from authorized labs and CTF environments.
- **Research:** experiments, methodology, and root-cause analysis.
- **Tools:** small scripts and utilities built for legitimate security testing.

## The approach

I believe a good security writeup should explain more than a payload. It should cover the underlying behavior, the conditions that make the issue possible, its impact, and the defensive lessons.

This is a learning journey. Expect detailed notes, honest experiments, and continuous improvement.

> Break things only where you have permission. Understand deeply. Share responsibly.
